# Economic ROI Optimization Framework & Mathematical Formulation

## Executive Overview — The Key Differentiating Factor

The primary element that distinguishes this project from conventional machine learning projects is the **Economic Decision Intelligence Engine**.

Conventional ML systems stop at Level 1 ($P(\text{Churn})$) or Level 2 (SHAP drivers). Our system connects predictions directly to balance-sheet financial metrics:

$$\text{Optimal Action} = \text{Prediction} + \text{Explanation} + \text{Customer Value} + \text{Intervention Cost} \implies \text{Optimal Business Action}$$

---

## Core Financial Equations

### 1. Annual Customer Value ($V_i$)
The gross annual monetary revenue generated by customer $i$:
$$V_i = \text{MonthlyCharges}_i \times 12$$

Alternatively, for long-term strategic retention, we utilize the 24-month estimated **Customer Lifetime Value ($\text{CLV}_i$)**.

---

### 2. Expected Retention Benefit ($\text{EB}_{i,a}$)
The expected monetary revenue saved by applying retention intervention $a$ to customer $i$:

$$\text{Expected Benefit}_{i,a} = V_i \times P(\text{Churn}_i) \times P(\text{Acceptance}_{i,a})$$

Where:
- $V_i$: Annual Customer Value ($) or CLV ($).
- $P(\text{Churn}_i)$: Calibrated XGBoost probability that customer $i$ will churn ($[0, 1]$).
- $P(\text{Acceptance}_{i,a})$: Expected Save / Acceptance Probability that customer $i$ accepts intervention $a$ and stays (e.g. 35% – 82%).

---

### 3. Expected Net Profit / Net ROI ($\text{EG}_{i,a}$)
The net financial gain after subtracting the intervention execution cost ($C_{\text{interv}}(a)$):

$$\text{Expected Net Gain}_{i,a} = \text{Expected Benefit}_{i,a} - C_{\text{interv}}(a)$$

$$\text{Expected Net Gain}_{i,a} = \left[ V_i \times P(\text{Churn}_i) \times P(\text{Acceptance}_{i,a}) \right] - C_{\text{interv}}(a)$$

---

### 4. Optimal Business Action Decision Logic ($a^*$)
For each individual customer $i$, the engine evaluates all candidate interventions $a \in \text{Catalog}$ and selects the intervention $a^*$ that maximizes **Expected Net Gain**:

$$a^* = \arg\max_{a} \text{Expected Net Gain}_{i,a}$$

**Decision Rule:**
- If $\max_a \text{Expected Net Gain}_{i,a} > 0 \implies \text{Execute Intervention } a^*$.
- If $\max_a \text{Expected Net Gain}_{i,a} \le 0 \implies \text{Prescribe } \text{NO\_ACTION}$ (Prevents negative ROI marketing spend).
